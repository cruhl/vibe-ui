# Held-frame judge questions

Each line is `key: text`. `before` is the frame on screen when the person pressed, `after` the held last frame of the clip that answered it. `{change}` is the one-line change the clip was meant to show, `{label}` the pressed element's printed words, `{look}` an element's look sentence.

changed: Compare `before` with `after`. Did the picture visibly change this way: {change}
changed-unknown: Compare `before` with `after`. Did the picture visibly change in response to choosing {label}?
present: Is this in `after`: {look}
legible: Is the printed text {label} clearly legible in `after`?
legibility: How legible is all the printed text in `after`?
legibility-0: Every word crisp and correctly spelled.
legibility-1: Most words readable, a few soft or misspelled.
legibility-2: Some words readable, many garbled.
legibility-3: Words are garbled shapes or unreadable.
