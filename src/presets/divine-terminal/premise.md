# Divine Terminal

A made-up 640 by 480, 16 color operating system in the style of TempleOS, built for God, shown as a full screen view of its interface filling the whole frame. You are God, and this is your terminal.

## Look

- Chunky low resolution pixels, as if a 640 by 480 screen is enlarged to fill the frame. Only the sixteen classic VGA colors: white, black, dark blue, light blue, cyan, red, yellow, green, magenta and gray.
- The background is plain white. A thin bar across the very top is dark blue with cyan blocky text showing a clock and the words DAY 7.
- Five big rectangular buttons are stacked in a column down the left half of the screen, each a solid block of color with a double line border and one word in small crisp blocky white pixel capitals, centered with even padding: CREATION (green), PRAYERS (dark blue), MIRACLES (yellow with black text), SMITE (red) and HYMNS (magenta).
- On the right half sits a small pixel sprite of a golden angel with white wings, hovering beside a tiny pixel globe.
- Windows are white rectangles with a double line border in dark blue, a title in blocky blue capitals along the top border, and a small red X in the top right corner.
- Design: one small crisp pixel font in capitals for every word, on a strict character grid, with even white space around each line, so the terminal looks precise and holy rather than crowded. Printed words are written in capitals.
- Text inside windows is blocky dark blue monospace capitals on white, with words in red and cyan for emphasis, like an old text terminal. Around the text, every window is alive with pixel action: things being created, answered, multiplied or struck by lightning.
- Everything is flat, front facing and evenly lit, like a real desktop screenshot. The camera never moves; what fills the screen is in constant motion until each clip comes to rest.

## Behavior

Every choice plays out as an act of God rendered in sixteen colors, a burst of pixel action that fills the clip and lands straight on the result. What opens is the living act itself, already underway, never an empty void, a blank inbox or a button that only leads to the real thing. The OS takes itself completely seriously, like a sacred instrument. It starts as dry system administration of the universe and escalates to cosmic absurdity, never winking.

- CREATION opens with creation already underway: light bursting out of a black void, pixel water rushing in and a single pixel duck paddling across it, with picks like LET THERE BE and AGAIN. Each pick creates something new in a flash of pixel light, and several things at once: whole forests sprouting, mountains rising, flocks of ducks. Later presses create oddly specific things: a parking garage, a second moon, a duck the size of a continent.
- PRAYERS opens onto a flood of prayers scrolling in from tiny pixel people across the globe, a lost set of keys, rain for a farm, a good parking spot, with picks like ANSWER and ANSWER ALL. Each pick shows the answer happening: keys drop from the sky into a hand, rain pours on a farm, a car glides into a parking spot, and green checkmarks pop up down the list. Later the prayers get stranger: a goldfish praying for a bigger bowl, a prayer from the angel sprite itself.
- MIRACLES opens with pixel loaves already multiplying, bread bouncing and stacking, with picks like MULTIPLY and FISH. Each pick doubles the miracle in one burst until bread and fish fill the whole window and spill onto the desktop, and later the loaves start multiplying the angel.
- SMITE opens with a pixel thundercloud already crackling over a small target, with picks like SMITE and BIGGER BOLT. Each pick hurls a huge blocky lightning bolt that only ever strikes harmless things in a shower of sparks: a parking ticket, a slow website, a sock with a hole.
- HYMNS opens with a hymn already playing, blocky title letters scrolling and a pixel choir of tiny angels singing, with picks like GENERATE and LOUDER. Each pick makes a new hymn title blaze across the window while the choir grows and the square wave organ plays a short tune.
- Choosing the angel sprite makes it flutter in a loop the loop and bow, scattering pixel sparkles.
- The red X closes a window and the white desktop returns.
- Jokes come from running all of existence through an earnest, homemade, sixteen color terminal.

## Sound

A beeping PC speaker for every click, square wave organ chords when a window opens, a low computer hum, and faint choir voices held on one long note far in the background.

## Windows

- This is a screen world: one window is open at a time, always in the `center` place. A new window added at `center` replaces the open one.
- A new window is a top-level `+` line at `center` with its look and its title as WORDS, followed by its close button at `title.right` (a small pick printed X), two to four picks inside it, and the living content that fills it, all in the same turn. A window arrives already full of the living result, alive and moving, never empty or waiting for a further step.
- Every window has a close button, and choosing it removes its window.
- A window that was open before comes back by its id with no look, and its insides come back by their ids too.
