# Kaiju Attack

A giant monster is attacking a city, filmed like a big colorful 1960s monster movie made with miniature models. A high fixed camera looks across the whole city from a rooftop: the monster towers over the skyline in the middle, and everything around it is something it can smash, eat, swat or fight. Choosing anything makes the monster do something huge to it, or makes it do something huge to the monster. The city responds with total seriousness and escalating firepower; the monster responds like an overgrown toddler having the best day of its life.

## Look

- A high fixed camera on a rooftop looks across the whole city in bright afternoon light, from the street in the near foreground to green hills and a bay behind the skyline. The camera stays fixed in place the whole time and only shakes when something huge lands.
- The monster towers in the middle of the city, its head and shoulders above the rooftops: a huge round purple monster with a crest of orange spikes down its back, tiny bat wings, stubby arms and a wide toothy grin.
- A tall blue glass skyscraper stands on the left side of the skyline.
- A green army tank sits on the street in the near right foreground, its cannon aimed up at the monster.
- A red and white news helicopter hovers in the sky on the right.
- A giant pink donut sign spins on a rooftop in the near left foreground.
- Lettering is rare: a station name on the helicopter or a sign on a building, in condensed 1960s sans capitals in crisp white, one or two words, small on its surface. Printed words are written in capitals.
- Layout: the monster leads in the middle, the skyscraper, the tank, the helicopter and the donut sign each keep their own corner of the frame with open sky and street between them.
- Everything is a slightly toy like miniature: crisp model buildings, tiny cars on the streets, painted clouds. Destruction is big, colorful and cartoonish: puffs of dust, sparkling rubble, bright fireballs, and everyone in the city stays perfectly fine.
- After every change the dust settles and every building, vehicle and creature holds its place, a clear steady view of the whole city.

## Behavior

Every choice bursts into a cascade of destruction and spectacle that fills the clip, visible across the whole frame: a building crumbles, the sky fills with fire, a tank flips upside down, a new giant arrives, and the city reacts all at once, crowds of tiny people running, sirens flashing, the news helicopter swooping in. Several things may change in one choice. Small reactions are too little. It starts as a classic monster movie attack and escalates: the army brings bigger and sillier weapons, a giant robot arrives to fight, a second monster rises from the bay, and finally the two monsters and the robot become best friends and the city throws them a parade. The city and the news crew play every moment completely straight and terrified.

- The change always lands on the chosen thing itself, in one of three ways. A thing that changes in place gets a new state. A thing that is destroyed is removed, and a new heap of rubble takes its spot. A thing that ends up somewhere else, such as held up in the monster's claws or stuck on its spikes, is removed and brought back by its id at its new spot on the monster, so it stops showing where it was. A thing that is eaten is removed, and the monster's state shows the evidence.
- Every state is the picture that holds once the dust settles, a big still pose seen from across the city, rather than an action in progress: the monster with both arms thrown up and its mouth wide open, a huge column of hot pink fire roaring straight up into the sky; the tank flipped upside down on its turret with its tracks spinning in the air; the helicopter perched on the monster's head; the monster grinning with pink frosting smeared all over its face.
- The monster: first it throws its arms up and roars a huge column of hot pink fire straight up into the sky, and holds that pose; later it stomps a giant crater into the street, dances until every tower leans, sneezes a tornado, or pounds its chest and grows even bigger. It speaks only in roars.
- The skyscraper: the monster smashes it with one huge fist and a heap of smoking blue glass rubble takes its place; a new building can rise in its place later, and the monster smashes that too, or wears it as a hat.
- The tank: it fires its cannon, the shell bonks off the monster's belly, and the monster flicks the tank over with one claw, leaving it flipped upside down on its turret with its tracks spinning in the air; later more tanks roll in, the monster juggles them, then a giant mech suit arrives.
- The helicopter: the monster swats it and it ends up perched crooked on top of the monster's head, rotor still spinning; later the monster holds it up to its face like a bug.
- The donut sign: the monster plucks the giant donut off the roof and eats it in one bite, so the donut is gone and the monster grins with pink frosting smeared all over its face; later a whole bakery truck, a stadium full of cotton candy, a giant ice cream cone on another roof.
- New arrivals crash in from the sky, rise from the bay or roll in down the streets: a giant silver robot, a second monster that is the first one's even bigger mother, a fleet of blimps, a squadron of jets dragging banners. They leave by being flung far over the hills in a puff of dust, and can come back later looking the same.

## Sound

Earth shaking footsteps, a huge rumbling roar, buildings crumbling, the thump of a tank cannon, helicopter blades chopping, air raid sirens wailing far off, and dramatic brass monster movie stabs.
