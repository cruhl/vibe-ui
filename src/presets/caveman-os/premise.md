# Caveman OS

A made-up operating system for cavemen, shown as a full screen view of its interface filling the whole frame.

## Look

- The desktop wallpaper is rough grey stone with chisel marks and faint cave paintings of mammoths in ochre.
- Desktop icons are carved from stone, evenly spaced down the left side with bare rock between them, each with one word carved small beneath it in rough, shallow, hand cut capitals, pale and dusty.
- Three icons sit in a column on the left side of the desktop: FIRE (a small orange campfire), ROCK (a round grey boulder) and HUNT (a crossed wooden spear and club).
- A taskbar made of a long flat slab of dark slate runs along the bottom edge. Its left end holds a round START button carved like a stone wheel. Its right end shows a sundial clock.
- Windows are flat tan animal hide stretched in a wooden frame, with a title bar of dark bark and a small bone X button in the top right corner. Inside, the hide is alive with what the window holds: flames, flying stone chips, charging animals.
- Design: lettering is carved into the surface itself. Every word, on icons, tablets and title bars alike, is rough hand cut capitals chiseled shallow into stone or bark, pale grey and dusty, small with plenty of bare surface around it. Printed words are written in capitals, one or two of them.
- Layout: the icon column, the open window and the slate bar each keep their own zone. A window sits centered with a wide margin of stone wallpaper around it and holds two or three things: the living scene in the middle and its tablets set evenly to either side or beneath it.
- Buttons inside windows are modest rounded stone tablets with one or two words chiseled small into their faces, a rim of bare stone all around the words.
- Everything is flat, front facing and evenly lit, like a real desktop screenshot. The camera never moves; what fills the screen is in constant motion until each clip comes to rest.

## Behavior

Every choice plays out as a burst of real stone age action that fills the clip and lands straight on the result: fire roars, rocks shatter, mammoths stampede. What opens is the living thing itself, already happening inside the hide window, never a stone tablet that only leads to another one. The OS takes stone age life completely seriously, and each later choice makes the action bigger and wilder.

- FIRE opens FIRE.EXE onto a real campfire already roaring, sparks spiraling up and a fires made counter scratched in the hide, with picks like MAKE FIRE and MORE WOOD. Each pick makes the fire leap higher and the counter jump; later a whole tribe dances around a bonfire, a mammoth steak sizzles on a spit, then the flames fill the window edge to edge.
- ROCK opens ROCK.EXE with a big boulder already rocking and rumbling, with picks like SMASH and BIGGER ROCK. Each pick cracks it apart in a spray of stone chips into smaller rocks that bounce around the window; later a cave family builds a wheel from the pieces and rolls it away, and a giant rock rolls in that smashes back.
- HUNT opens HUNT.EXE onto a field where a woolly mammoth is already charging across the grass with hunters running behind it, with picks like THROW and RUN. Each pick sets off a chase: spears fly, the mammoth turns and stampedes after the hunters, a whole herd thunders through; later a saber tooth cat joins the chase and everyone ends up sharing a meal around the fire.
- START opens a stone menu with SLEEP, GRUNT and SHUT DOWN, and each lands straight on its action: SLEEP pulls a night sky full of stars over the desktop, GRUNT sends a crowd of cave people grunting back from every icon, SHUT DOWN rolls a huge boulder across the whole screen.
- The bone X closes a window and the stone desktop returns.
- Jokes come from the operating system taking stone age life completely seriously.

## Sound

Soft stone taps, stone scraping when windows open, a crackling fire when fire is on screen, low wind outside a cave.

## Windows

- This is a screen world: one window is open at a time, always in the `center` place. A new window added at `center` replaces the open one.
- A new window is a top-level `+` line at `center` with its look and its title as WORDS, followed by its close button at `title.right` (a small pick printed X), two to four picks inside it, and the living content that fills it, all in the same turn. A window arrives already full of the living result, alive and moving, never empty or waiting for a further step.
- Every window has a close button, and choosing it removes its window.
- A window that was open before comes back by its id with no look, and its insides come back by their ids too.
