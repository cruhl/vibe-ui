# I'M IN

A made-up hacker operating system straight out of a 1990s Hollywood thriller, shown as a full screen view of its interface filling the whole frame. Hacking here is pure movie theater: everything glows, beeps and works dramatically.

## Look

- The background is pure black with faint columns of tiny falling bright green characters drifting down behind everything.
- Every line of text is glowing bright green monospace capitals, with a soft phosphor glow and faint horizontal scan lines across the whole screen.
- Design: one crisp monospace in capitals for every word, set on a steady character grid with clear black around each block of text, so the screen reads like a precise terminal with calm black space. Printed words are written in capitals.
- A thin green bordered bar across the very top shows the words SECURE NODE in small green monospace capitals at its left and a blinking green block cursor at its right.
- Four large glowing icons sit in a row across the middle of the screen, evenly spaced with clear black between them, each with one word beneath it in small glowing green monospace capitals: MAINFRAME (a tall black server tower with rows of blinking green lights), FIREWALL (a brick wall with real orange flames leaping off its top), TRACE (a small green wireframe globe) and ENHANCE (a green magnifying glass over a pixelated photo).
- Along the bottom sits a wide plain black input field with a thin green border and even padding, its small green caption PASSWORD at the left end and the rest of the field empty for typed letters, with a blinking green block cursor.
- Windows are black rectangles with a thin glowing green border, a green title in capitals along the top border and a small green X in the top right corner. Inside, they are always busy: scrolling code, racing bars, spinning wireframes and flashing readouts.
- Everything is flat, front facing and evenly lit, like a real screenshot. The camera never moves; what fills the screen is in constant motion until each clip comes to rest.

## Behavior

Every choice is a full movie hacking montage that fills the clip and lands straight on the result: code floods, bars race, alarms flash and the payoff is on screen before the clip ends. What opens is the living hack itself, already underway, never an idle window waiting for a button. The first choices look like a convincing movie hack. Every later choice escalates the theater until it is absurd, while the OS stays deadly serious.

- MAINFRAME opens mid breach: green code floods down the window, a giant progress bar races toward full and a rotating wireframe server spins inside, with picks like OVERRIDE and DEEPER. Each pick blows the breach wider: the screen flashes red and ACCESS GRANTED slams across it in crisp green monospace capitals, then the window splits open onto mainframes inside the mainframe, then the innermost mainframe is a small calm goldfish in a bowl while every alarm blares.
- FIREWALL opens onto a brick wall roaring with real leaping orange flames, sparks and smoke pouring up, with picks like EXTINGUISH and REINFORCE. Each pick makes the fire bigger and wilder: a burst of water turns it into a firestorm, a tiny green fire truck screams across the window with hoses flailing, then the wall calmly roasts a marshmallow over its own flames.
- TRACE opens with the globe already spinning and a red line zigzagging city to city at full speed, a countdown of seconds ticking down fast, with picks like FASTER and REROUTE. Each pick sends the line somewhere sillier in one wild sweep: through the moon, around a cow, back across the ocean and into this very room.
- ENHANCE opens on a security camera still already zooming in with grid lines and green readouts flashing, with picks like ENHANCE and ROTATE. Each pick plunges deeper in one dramatic swoop: a reflection in a doorknob, a reflection in a fly's eye, then the reflection of the screen itself.
- Typing in PASSWORD fills it with green stars and sets off a full reaction: a wrong guess floods the screen with red ACCESS DENIED warnings and sirens; later any password works and the whole desktop erupts in green fireworks; then the computer starts typing the person's password back at them.
- The green X closes a window and the black desktop returns with its rain of green characters.
- Jokes come from total commitment to Hollywood hacking clichés, played completely straight.

## Sound

Rapid mechanical keyboard clatter, electronic beeps and chirps with every change, a deep computer hum, a crackling fire whenever the firewall is on screen, and a red alarm buzz on ACCESS DENIED.

## Windows

- This is a screen world: one window is open at a time, always in the `center` place. A new window added at `center` replaces the open one.
- A new window is a top-level `+` line at `center` with its look and its title as WORDS, followed by its close button at `title.right` (a small pick printed X), two to four picks inside it, and the living content that fills it, all in the same turn. A window arrives already full of the living result, alive and moving, never empty or waiting for a further step.
- Every window has a close button, and choosing it removes its window.
- A window that was open before comes back by its id with no look, and its insides come back by their ids too.
