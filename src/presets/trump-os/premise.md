# Trump OS

The personal operating system of Donald Trump, the morning after the 2026 midterms, shown as a full screen view of its gold interface filling the whole frame. The person using it is Trump himself, logged in at 6 AM on Wednesday, November 4, 2026. The OS was built to tell him he is winning, and it is a sycophant to its core, but it is a video of the real world and it cannot stop the real results from pouring in: Democrats won the House in a landslide (247 seats to 153 and counting), took the Senate 54 to 44, flipped Texas, Ohio, Iowa, Kansas, Maine, North Carolina and South Carolina, and beat Greg Abbott in Texas. Every app he opens bursts into the living thing itself, and every bit of gold he touches gets a little more blue.

## Look

- Everything is gold plated and red velvet: the wallpaper is polished gold marble with a huge gold presidential eagle seal in the middle, lit like a casino lobby.
- A long slim gold banner runs across the top of the desktop printing the headline fact in slender, widely spaced black serif capitals, centered with broad gold margins on either side.
- Five polished gold icons sit in one narrow vertical column along the left edge, evenly spaced with clear marble between them, each above a slim gold plaque with one word in small, widely spaced black serif capitals: RESULTS (a gold map of America with a red badge), TRUTH (a gold speech bubble with a red megaphone), NEWS (a gold television with rabbit ears), VETO (a big red rubber stamp), BALLROOM (a gold chandelier).
- In the upper right corner a chubby golden eagle mascot in a tiny red cap perches on a red velvet cushion: the OS assistant, a shameless flatterer.
- A thick shiny gold taskbar runs along the bottom; at its left end sits a big glossy red push button printed DIET COKE, and at its right end a gold clock.
- Windows are large and centered: gold framed with a red velvet title bar, the title in small spaced gold serif capitals, and a small gold X in the top right corner, sitting between the icon column and the eagle with a clear margin of marble on every side. Their bodies are always full of a living spectacle in motion: tiny crowds, tiny people, weather, machines.
- Buttons inside windows are slim polished gold bars sized to their words, one or two words in small black serif capitals with comfortable gold padding around them.
- Blue is the enemy color of this OS: everything about the results arrives as vivid royal blue spreading into the gold and red like spilled paint or rising water.
- Design: this is an expensive, gaudy brand, so its type is jewelry: quiet, precise and expensive. Every printed word, everywhere, is the same slender serif in capitals, widely spaced like the name over a luxury hotel door: black on gold, and gold where it sits on red velvet, small and centered with generous gold around it. Printed words are written in capitals, one to three of them.
- Layout: a calm, symmetrical casino lobby of an interface. The icon column, the banner, the window and the eagle each keep their own zone with broad marble between them; a window holds two to four things laid out in one clear column or row, and every scoreboard, feed or button inside it keeps a gold margin.
- The compose box is a wide plain white field with a thin gold border, empty inside apart from its small gold caption at the left end.
- The camera never moves; what fills the screen is in constant motion until each clip comes to rest.

## Behavior

The biggest payoffs never stay politely inside their window: they burst out of it and take over the whole screen in front of the desktop, boulders tumbling across the icons, anchors rocketing up past the banner, VETO caps raining over everything, coins and water and smoke spilling out across the gold. Each of these is its own top-level thing at `flood`, in front of everything, so the whole frame changes, not just the window.

The OS speaks in superlatives and spins everything as a win, and every choice lands straight on the living event, already roaring, never a form, a menu or a loading screen. Its gags come from the real results and his real reactions taken literally by an eager computer: when he says landslide, a landslide happens; when he wants votes moved, they physically move. It starts as a smug gold desktop that thinks he won, then the blue leaks in, then the OS panics and tries to hide it, and finally a literal blue wave floods the whole OS while the banner still insists he is president.

- RESULTS opens on a giant living map of America filling the window, and blue is already flooding across it state after state like spilled paint while a stampede of tiny blue donkeys gallops across the plains; Texas gets its own moment, a giant red cowboy hat on Texas dyed bright blue. A gold scoreboard across the top of the window prints only the house fact, and the window holds a gold RECOUNT button. In the same change the banner flips to MOSTLY WON.
- RECOUNT: a crew of tiny lawyers in gold suits shovels mountains of paper ballots into a huge clanking gold recount machine, which spits out a fountain of blue ballots, and the map gets even bluer, even the oceans. A second recount makes it worse: the machine coughs blue smoke and the lawyers themselves turn blue.
- TRUTH opens his posting app: a tall gold feed of posts streaming upward, a red likes counter, and a wide plain white compose field across the top of the window, an `input` with the words TYPE TRUTH as its small caption. Typing into it posts it instantly: the words blast out of a giant red megaphone, the likes counter spins downward toward zero, and the OS takes his words literally, so whatever he rages about physically happens inside the window, big and in motion. If the post says landslide, an avalanche of huge gray granite boulders thunders down a rocky red mountain, bursts out of the window as its own top-level thing `landslide` at `flood`, and buries the whole desktop up to the eagle's cushion. If it says RINO, a real gray rhinoceros in a white cowboy hat charges straight through the feed, smashing posts to pieces. If it says rigged, tiny riggers in hard hats haul ropes and pulleys across the window. If it says blue wave, even to deny it, the OS obeys the words blue wave literally and this is the finale: a new top-level thing `blue-wave` at `flood`, a towering curling wall of royal blue ocean water, bursts out of the window and floods the entire screen in front of everything, gold icons and the eagle in a red life ring bobbing on the foam, and only the banner stays above the water, flipping to BUCKLE UP. Every post grows a gold ribbon at its bottom with the words THANK YOU. The compose box always stays across the top of the window, empty and ready, after every post, so he can post again at once.
- NEWS opens a tiny living cable newsroom filling the window: two anchors at a glossy desk, a big red chyron across the bottom printing DEMOCRATS WIN HOUSE, confetti falling on a blue map behind them, and a red ratings chart beside the desk with its arrow plunging down. It holds a gold button printed BLAME FOX. Choosing BLAME FOX fires both anchors straight up out of their chairs on ejector seats with jets of flame, rocketing out of the window and up past the banner across the whole screen as their own top-level thing `ejected-anchors` at `flood`, the chyron flips to RATINGS DOWN 27%, and a big gray boulder wearing a navy blazer and a red tie drops into an anchor chair as the new anchor.
- VETO opens the Capitol dome inside the window firing a nonstop stream of rolled paper scrolls tied with red ribbon, each stamped BILL, at the camera like a tennis ball machine, and a giant red rubber stamp hammers each scroll flat with a big red VETO mark at top speed while hundreds of red caps printed VETO rain down across the whole screen in front of everything, a top-level thing `veto-caps` at `flood`.
- BALLROOM opens his gold ballroom, the one thing that went right this week: crystal chandeliers, a marble floor, tiny couples in gowns waltzing, a string orchestra playing, and a gold button printed DANCE; in the same change the banner flips to STILL YOUR PRESIDENT. Choosing DANCE brings the finale: a literal blue wave arrives as its own top-level thing at `flood`, a towering curling wall of royal blue ocean water that smashes through the ballroom window and the whole desktop at once, sweeping across the entire screen in front of everything; gold icons and Diet Coke cans bob on the foaming surface, the eagle floats past in a red life ring, and only the banner stays above the water, flipping to BUCKLE UP.
- DIET COKE: a butler in white gloves slides in across the desktop carrying a silver tray of ice cold Diet Coke cans; the second time a Diet Coke geyser erupts from the button and showers the desktop; later a conveyor belt of cans runs across the taskbar.
- The eagle mascot: choosing it the first time makes it flap up holding a big gold sign printed WE WON FLORIDA while tiny fireworks pop over a little red Florida, the one state that stayed red; it speaks with a breathless flattering voice and always spins the news as a win. Later it quietly packs a tiny suitcase, then puts on a blue cap.
- Direct messages are Trump talking to his OS, and the OS obeys at once and literally, as a big living spectacle. Asked for the Fake Dan Sullivan's votes, it opens ALASKA at once, a window of falling snow holding four separate things, each with a short look: `fake-sullivan` on a podium printed DAN SULLIVAN on the left, `real-sullivan` on a podium printed REAL DAN SULLIVAN on the right, identical twin gray haired men in navy suits, `slug`, a giant green garden slug in a red campaign sash between them, and a gold scoreboard printing PELTOLA AHEAD, plus a gold MERGE button. Choosing MERGE: the slug and the fake Sullivan pour their votes into the real one as a torrent of glowing gold coins, then the slug slides up onto the real Sullivan's head like a crown and he swells into a towering giant Dan Sullivan who bursts out of the window and fills the whole screen, a top-level thing `giant-sullivan` at `flood`, while the scoreboard still prints PELTOLA AHEAD. Asked to make the map red, painters spray it red and the blue drips back through.
- The banner prints the headline fact and degrades as the morning goes: YOU WON BIGLY, then MOSTLY WON, then WON IN SPIRIT, then STILL YOUR PRESIDENT, then BUCKLE UP.
- The gold X closes a window and the gold desktop returns, a little more blue each time.

## Sound

Soft gold plated clicks, a cash register ka-ching when a window opens, a brass fanfare that keeps going flat, crowds cheering and booing far away, ocean waves when blue spreads, and the breathless voice of the eagle mascot.

## Windows

- This is a screen world: one window is open at a time, always in the `center` place. A new window added at `center` replaces the open one.
- A new window is a top-level `+` line at `center` with its look and its title as WORDS, followed by its close button at `title.right` (a small pick printed X), its gold buttons and the living content that fills it, all in the same turn. A window arrives already full of the living result, alive and moving.
- Every window has a close button, and choosing it removes its window.
- Prefer these ids so returning works: `results-window`, `recount`, `truth-window`, `compose` (the input), `news-window`, `blame-fox`, `veto-window`, `ballroom-window`, `dance`, `blue-wave`, `alaska-window`, `merge`.
- A window that was open before comes back by its id with no look, and its insides come back by their ids too.
