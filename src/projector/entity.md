# Entity projection phrasing

Each line is `key: text`. `{name}` is filled by the projector. A `the` form turns an entity's look from "a stone button" into "the stone button". Printed words are copied exactly as the world writes them, in its own case. The world's typeface, `{lettering}`, is named once for every word in view, so the whole frame shares one typographic voice, and the composition sentence gives every thing and label its breathing room so each sits in proportion to what carries it. This file holds only phrasing that fits every kind of world; anything that depends on how a world is filmed or moves (the opening hold, how a chosen thing is approached, how a top-level thing arrives or leaves, the settle in the last second) is the world's own canon. Every sentence describes what is there; nothing names a pointer or what is absent.

A clip is a burst of action: the approach takes the first moment, every change of the turn follows in order as one cascade, the beats keep everything moving through the middle, and only the canon's settle brings the world to rest in the last second, so the held end frame stays steady.
